About

From Bringup to System Debug

I've spent the past 5+ years building deep expertise in post-silicon hardware validation—starting from foundational lab bringup and measurement procedures, then growing into independent ownership of full characterization flows across multiple silicon generations.

My work has centered on high-speed memory interface characterization, including DDR4/5/6, JEDEC compliance testing, and SerDes validation. I've driven complex system debug tasks spanning calibration flows, timing mismatches, jitter behaviors, and training algorithms—working across software, hardware, and instrument interactions to deliver actionable insights.

Alongside validation, I've continuously built automation—from extending test scripts and adding hardware integration, to developing multi-threaded frameworks and real-time visualization tools. That combination of hands-on lab depth and software-driven efficiency is what defines my approach to engineering.

Development of new loss and impedance evaluation techniques.

Cross-Functional Collaboration
